# PAA Research Guide

## What Is PAA?

People Also Ask (PAA) boxes appear in Google search results as expandable question-answer pairs. Clicking any question expands its answer (drawn from a featured snippet) and generates new related questions, creating an infinite expansion tree.

PAA appears for approximately 70-80% of searches and typically shows 4 initial questions, expanding to 10+ when clicked.

## Why PAA Matters for AEO

- Each PAA answer is its own zero-click placement
- PAA questions reveal exactly what users want to know about a topic
- Capturing multiple PAA answers for one topic signals topical authority
- PAA answers feed into voice assistants and AI engines
- PAA positions are more stable than featured snippets (less competition for each question)

## PAA Discovery Methods

### Method 1: Manual Google Extraction

1. Search your primary keyword in an incognito browser window
2. Note all 4 PAA questions visible without clicking
3. Click each question to expand it, revealing 4 new questions
4. Click the new questions to see another tier
5. Repeat until you have 20-40 questions
6. Document all questions in a spreadsheet

**Time required:** 15-20 minutes per primary keyword
**Best for:** Deep topic research, content outline building

### Method 2: AlsoAsked Tool

Website: alsoasked.com

1. Enter your keyword
2. Tool maps the entire PAA tree visually
3. Export as CSV or image
4. Filter by question type (What, How, Why, When, Who)

**Time required:** 2-3 minutes
**Best for:** Quick PAA research, visual topic mapping

### Method 3: AnswerThePublic

Website: answerthepublic.com

1. Enter your seed keyword
2. Generates hundreds of question variations organized by question word
3. Includes PAA-style questions plus preposition and comparison queries
4. Export to CSV for prioritization

**Best for:** Content planning, finding long-tail question variants

### Method 4: Semrush Keyword Magic Tool

Filter by "Questions" in Keyword Magic Tool to surface question-format keywords with search volume data. Cross-reference with PAA manual research to prioritize by volume.

### Method 5: Google Search Console

In GSC > Performance > Queries, filter by queries containing:
- "what", "how", "why", "when", "who", "which", "can", "should", "does", "is", "are"

These are likely PAA-eligible queries your site may already appear for. Optimize pages targeting these queries first for quick wins.

## Prioritizing PAA Questions

Score each PAA question on:

| Criterion | Weight | Score (1-5) |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance to your content | 30% | |
| Search volume (if data available) | 25% | |
| Answer difficulty (how easy to answer well) | 20% | |
| Competitor answer quality (worse = opportunity) | 15% | |
| Conversion intent (BOFU = higher priority) | 10% | |

Weighted total = PAA Priority Score. Target top 10 first.

## PAA Content Formatting Rules

Each PAA answer must:

1. **Start with the keyword/topic** ("Machine learning is..." not "It is...")
2. **Be 40-80 words** (Google prefers this range for PAA)
3. **Be written in full sentences** (no fragments, no bullet points in the answer itself)
4. **Stand alone contextually** (the reader sees only this answer, not surrounding content)
5. **Avoid the first person** ("the process" not "our process")
6. **Avoid marketing language** ("effective solution" will lose to factual answers)

## PAA Tracking and Monitoring

### Manual Tracking (Free)

Create a Google Sheet with columns:
- PAA Question
- Target page URL
- Date first appeared in our PAA
- Current answer source (our domain or competitor?)
- Answer preview (first 50 chars of our answer)

Check weekly by searching the parent keyword and noting PAA box content.

### Tool-Based Tracking

- **SERanking** -- Tracks PAA positions and changes over time
- **Semrush Position Tracking** -- Features PAA monitoring
- **Ahrefs** -- Shows SERP features including PAA for tracked keywords
- **Rank Math (WordPress)** -- Shows PAA questions for individual posts

### Success Metrics

- **PAA capture rate** -- % of target PAA questions where your domain is the answer source
- **PAA impression share** -- Total estimated impressions from PAA appearances
- **Time to capture** -- Days from content publish to PAA appearance
- **Retention rate** -- % of captured PAA positions held at 90 days
