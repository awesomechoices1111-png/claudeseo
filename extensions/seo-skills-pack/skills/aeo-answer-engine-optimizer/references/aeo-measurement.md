# AEO Measurement and Reporting

## Core AEO Metrics

### Featured Snippet Metrics

| Metric | Definition | How to Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Snippet capture rate | % of target queries with your snippet | Manual checks or rank tracker | 15-30% of targets |
| Snippet impressions | Estimated eyeballs on your snippet | GSC impressions for snippet queries | Increasing MoM |
| Position zero CTR | Click-through from your snippet | GSC CTR for rank 0 positions | 8-25% (varies by topic) |
| Snippet retention | % of captured snippets held at 90 days | Tracker snapshot comparison | 70%+ |

### PAA Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| PAA coverage | # of PAA questions your domain answers | 20+ per core topic |
| PAA capture rate | % of target PAA Qs answered by your domain | 25-40% |
| PAA impressions | Estimated monthly PAA views | Growing trend |

### Voice Search Metrics

Voice search is difficult to measure directly. Proxy metrics:
- Featured snippet impressions for conversational queries (30+ word questions)
- Rankings for "near me" queries (if local)
- Google Assistant actions or Bing Copilot appearances (enterprise tools)

## Measurement Tools

### Free Tools

**Google Search Console**
Primary source for AEO measurement:
- Performance > Search Results > Filter by "Position" to identify snippet positions
- Filter queries by question words (what, how, why) to isolate AEO opportunities
- Compare clicks and impressions for position 0 vs position 1 to measure snippet value
- Check if impressions increased after FAQ schema implementation

Setup: Add property, verify ownership, wait 3-4 days for initial data.

**Google Search (Manual)**
- Search each target query in incognito
- Record whether your domain appears in featured snippet, PAA, or neither
- Log in a tracking spreadsheet weekly

### Paid Tools

**SE Ranking**
- Tracks SERP features including featured snippets and PAA
- Shows history of snippet wins and losses
- Alerts when you gain or lose a snippet
- Starting price: ~$55/month

**Semrush**
- Position Tracking with SERP feature filters
- Keyword Magic Tool shows PAA questions for any seed keyword
- Site Audit identifies structured data issues
- Starting price: ~$140/month

**Ahrefs**
- Organic Keywords report filtered by SERP features
- Shows featured snippet opportunities (queries where a competitor has the snippet, not you)
- Site Audit for schema issues
- Starting price: ~$99/month

**AccuRanker**
- Real-time rank tracking with SERP feature monitoring
- Good for agencies tracking multiple clients
- Starting price: ~$116/month

## Reporting Template

### Monthly AEO Performance Report

**Summary Metrics (Month-over-Month)**
- Featured snippets owned: [X] (was [Y] last month, [+/-Z] change)
- PAA positions owned: [X] (was [Y], [+/-Z] change)
- AEO-attributed impressions: [X] (from GSC)
- AEO-attributed clicks: [X] (from GSC)
- Average snippet CTR: [X]%

**New Captures This Month**
List of queries where you newly won a featured snippet or PAA position.

**Lost Positions This Month**
List of queries where a competitor took your snippet. Include their URL and visible answer text for analysis.

**Top Opportunities (Not Yet Captured)**
Top 10 queries where competitor has the snippet but you are in position 2-5. These are next month's priorities.

**Actions Taken**
Schema implementations, content rewrites, new FAQ sections published. Correlate with any metric changes.

## Setting AEO Benchmarks

### For New Sites (0-12 months old)

Realistic targets:
- Month 3: First 1-3 featured snippets on long-tail queries
- Month 6: 5-15 featured snippets, 10-25 PAA answers
- Month 12: 20-50 featured snippets, 50+ PAA answers

### For Established Sites (1+ years, established rankings)

Realistic targets:
- Month 1: Audit all existing PAA opportunities and optimize top 20 pages
- Month 3: 25-50 new PAA answers captured, 10-20 snippets improved
- Month 6: 50+ incremental PAA answers, measurable impression growth in GSC

### Snippet CTR by Query Type

Use these benchmarks to evaluate your snippet performance:

| Query Type | Average Snippet CTR | Strong Performance |
|---|---|---|
| Brand queries | 40-60% | 60%+ |
| Informational queries | 8-15% | 20%+ |
| How-to queries | 10-20% | 25%+ |
| Comparison queries | 5-12% | 15%+ |
| Definition queries | 5-10% | 12%+ |

Low CTR on a snippet does not necessarily mean failure -- it may mean high zero-click satisfaction (user got their answer). Brand building value remains.
