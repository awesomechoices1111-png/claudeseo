# GBP Optimization Guide

Deep-dive reference for Google Business Profile optimization. Load this file when you need field-level guidance, category-specific rules, or advanced GBP features.

---

## Business Name Rules

Google's guidelines are strict. Violations can trigger suspensions.

**Allowed:**
- Legal business name or commonly known DBA
- Location identifiers only if they are part of the real-world name (e.g., "Downtown Austin Dental")

**Not allowed:**
- Adding keywords to pad the name ("Austin Dental — Best Dentist Teeth Whitening")
- Adding city name if it is not part of the real business name
- Using a practitioner name instead of the business name (unless solo practice)

**Practitioner listings:** Individual practitioners (doctors, lawyers, agents) may have their own listings alongside the business listing, provided they each serve customers independently.

## Category Selection Strategy

The primary category is the single most important local ranking signal on GBP. Choose the most specific category that accurately describes the business's primary service.

### Finding the Right Category

1. Search Google for a top competitor — click their GBP panel — the first category shown is usually ideal
2. Use the GBP category tool: in the profile editor, type the most specific term and select from autocomplete
3. Do not create your own categories — only choose from Google's approved list

### Secondary Categories

Add up to 9 secondary categories. Each secondary category can unlock additional GBP features (menus for restaurants, appointment links for healthcare, etc.).

**Priority approach:**
- List services in order of revenue contribution
- Only add categories for services you actively provide
- Avoid adding broad categories (e.g., "Local Business") — they dilute signals

### Category-Specific Features Unlocked

| Category Type | Additional GBP Features |
|--------------|------------------------|
| Restaurant/Food | Menu, food ordering, reserve table |
| Healthcare | Health insurance networks, telehealth |
| Hotel/Lodging | Star ratings, amenities, check-in info |
| Automotive | Services offered, brands serviced |
| Beauty/Salon | Services menu, booking |
| Legal | Practice areas, consultation booking |

## Business Description Best Practices

The description is 750 characters maximum and appears in the Knowledge Panel. It does not directly influence rankings but affects click-through and conversion.

**Structure:**
1. First sentence: What the business does + city (keyword-rich)
2. Middle sentences: Key differentiators, years of experience, specializations
3. Final sentence: Call to action or unique value proposition

**Example (Plumber, Austin TX):**
> "Austin Pipe Pros has served Austin homeowners with fast, reliable plumbing services since 2008. We specialize in emergency plumbing repairs, water heater installation, drain cleaning, and whole-home repiping. Our licensed plumbers offer same-day appointments across Austin and surrounding areas. Call us anytime — we're available 24/7 for urgent plumbing needs."

**Do not include:**
- URLs (Google strips them)
- Phone numbers
- Hours (use the hours field instead)
- Promotional language or sales copy

## Photo Optimization

Photos are a key engagement signal. Profiles with 100+ photos get significantly more calls and direction requests than those with fewer than 10.

### Photo Specifications

| Type | Recommended Size | Format | Notes |
|------|-----------------|--------|-------|
| Cover | 1332 x 750px | JPG/PNG | Appears at top of panel |
| Logo | 250 x 250px | JPG/PNG | Square, clear background |
| Interior | 720 x 540px min | JPG | Show the real environment |
| Exterior | 720 x 540px min | JPG | From street view angle |
| Product | 720 x 720px | JPG | Clean background preferred |
| Team | 720 x 540px | JPG | Professional but approachable |

### Photo Naming Convention

Upload photos with descriptive filenames before uploading (Google may index alt text):
- `austin-dental-office-interior.jpg` (better than `IMG_4821.jpg`)

### Video Specifications
- Max 30 seconds
- Max 75 MB
- Min 720p resolution
- No promotional overlays

## GBP Posts Guide

GBP Posts appear in the Knowledge Panel and Search results. They have a short shelf life (7 days for standard posts) so consistency matters.

### Post Types

| Type | Best For | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| Update | General news, tips, blog content | 7 days |
| Offer | Promotions, discount codes | Custom dates |
| Event | Classes, open days, community events | Until event date |
| Product | New product highlight | Stays on profile |

### Post Best Practices

- **Image:** Always include a 1200x900px image — posts with images get 3-5x more views
- **Text:** 150-300 characters optimal; first 100 characters appear before "Read more" cutoff
- **CTA:** Always add a button — "Learn More", "Book", "Order Online", "Call Now"
- **Keywords:** Naturally include the primary service and city in every post
- **Frequency:** At minimum 1 Update post per week; Offers and Events as needed

### Post Content Calendar (Monthly Framework)

| Week | Post Type | Topic Example |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | Update | Tip or educational content in your niche |
| 2 | Offer | Current promotion or seasonal deal |
| 3 | Update | Customer success story or testimonial (no names without permission) |
| 4 | Event / Product | Upcoming event or featured product/service |

## Q&A Management

The Q&A section is publicly editable — anyone can ask or answer questions. Left unmanaged, competitors or spammers may add incorrect information.

### Pre-Seeding FAQs

Log into your GBP, go to the Q&A section, and post questions yourself (from a personal Google account), then answer them from your business account. Target questions that:

1. Address buying objections ("Do you offer financing?")
2. Clarify service scope ("Do you serve [suburb]?")
3. Target keywords ("What types of [service] do you offer?")
4. Provide practical info ("Is parking available?")
5. Build trust ("Are you licensed and insured?")

### Q&A Monitoring

Enable notifications for new Q&A activity in GBP settings. Respond to all unanswered questions within 24 hours. Flag and report inaccurate answers submitted by others.

## Attributes Reference

Attributes appear as icons and tags on your GBP listing. They influence visibility for filtered searches (e.g., "wheelchair accessible restaurants near me").

### Universal Attributes (All Categories)

- Accessibility: Wheelchair accessible entrance/parking/seating/restroom
- Planning: Appointment required, walk-ins welcome
- Payments: Credit/debit cards accepted, NFC mobile payments, cash only

### Service Attributes

- Identifies as women-led, Black-owned, veteran-led, LGBTQ+-friendly
- On-site services available
- Online services available
- Online appointments available

### Restaurant Attributes (Food)

- Dine-in / Takeaway / Delivery / Curbside pickup
- Good for kids / Groups / Vegetarians
- Has live music / Sports bar / Rooftop seating
- Reservations required / accepted

### Healthcare Attributes

- Online care / Telehealth available
- Insurance types accepted
- Languages spoken
- Specialties listed

## Advanced GBP Features

### Messaging

Enable messaging to allow customers to contact the business via Google Search and Maps. Best practice: respond within 1 hour during business hours. Google may disable messaging if response time exceeds 24 hours.

Setup: GBP Dashboard > Messages > Turn on

### Booking Integration

Link a third-party booking system (Reserve with Google partners include Square, Booksy, Mindbody, Acuity, etc.) or add a custom booking URL. Booking links appear as a prominent CTA button on the GBP panel.

### Service Menu Builder

For applicable categories, build out a service menu directly in GBP:
- Each service gets a name, description, and optional price
- Services appear in a dedicated tab on the Knowledge Panel
- Use service keywords naturally in descriptions

### Products Catalog

Physical product businesses can add a product catalog:
- Photo, name, price range, description, CTA link per product
- Products appear in a carousel on the profile
- Ideal for retail, food products, or any tangible goods

## Suspension Prevention Checklist

GBP suspensions are increasingly common. Avoid these triggers:

- [ ] Business name contains keywords not in the real-world name
- [ ] Address is a virtual office, UPS store, or co-working space
- [ ] Multiple listings for the same business at the same address
- [ ] Listing uses a tracking phone number as the primary number
- [ ] Business category does not match actual business activity
- [ ] Profile was recently bulk-edited via API
- [ ] New listing created shortly after a previous suspension

If suspended: Do not create a new listing. Use the GBP support chat to submit a reinstatement request. Expect 3-10 business days for review.
