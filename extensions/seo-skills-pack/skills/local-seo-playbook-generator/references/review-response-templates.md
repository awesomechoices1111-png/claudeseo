# Review Response Templates — Extended Library

Extended template library for review responses across tones, industries, and scenarios. Load this file when the main playbook templates need additional variants or when calibrating tone for a specific business type.

---

## Tone Calibration Guide

Select the appropriate tone based on business category:

| Category | Tone | Key Traits |
|----------|------|------------|
| Medical / Legal / Financial | Professional / Warm | Formal grammar, empathetic, no slang, privacy-aware |
| Restaurants / Retail / Hospitality | Friendly / Casual | Conversational, upbeat, first names, personality |
| Home Services / Trades | Reliable / Down-to-earth | Straightforward, trustworthy, solution-focused |
| Beauty / Wellness / Fitness | Warm / Enthusiastic | Personal, energetic, community-focused |
| B2B / Professional Services | Confident / Respectful | Business-appropriate, outcome-focused |
| Education / Childcare | Caring / Reassuring | Warm, parent-friendly, safety-conscious |

## Positive Review Templates — Extended

### P4 — First-Time Customer

> Thank you for visiting us for the first time, [Name]! We're so glad your first experience with [Business Name] was a great one. We look forward to seeing you again — and if there's ever anything we can do better, please let us know.
>
> — [Business Name]

### P5 — Returning Customer Mentioned

> [Name], it means the world to us to have loyal customers like you! We genuinely appreciate your continued support of [Business Name]. [Add personalized line if specific service mentioned.] See you again soon!
>
> — [Team at Business Name]

### P6 — Specific Staff Member Mentioned

> Thank you so much, [Name]! We'll be sure to pass your kind words along to [Staff Name] — feedback like this really makes their day. We're lucky to have such a wonderful team, and even luckier to have customers like you.
>
> — [Manager Name], [Business Name]

### P7 — Response Mentioning a Specific Service (SEO-optimized)

> Thank you, [Name]! We're thrilled that our [specific service, e.g., "deep tissue massage"] hit the mark for you. Providing exceptional [service type] in [city] is what we're all about. We'd love to see you back at [Business Name] soon!
>
> — [Business Name]

**Note:** Template P7 is the most useful for local SEO — naturally weaves in service and location keywords. Use on public review platforms (Google, Yelp).

### P8 — Response When No Name Given

> Thank you so much for this wonderful review! It's always great to hear that our team is delivering the experience we work so hard to create. We hope to see you again at [Business Name] very soon!
>
> — [Business Name] Team

Feel free to reach out at [contact] anytime.

## Negative Review Templates — Extended

### G4 — Competitor or Wrong Business

> Thank you for your review. We checked our records and were unable to find a visit matching the details you described, which suggests this review may be intended for another business. If you did visit us and had a less-than-great experience, we would genuinely like to hear more. Please contact us directly at [email/phone] and we'll do everything we can to help.
>
> — [Business Name]

### G5 — Staff Behavior Complaint

> Thank you for bringing this to our attention, [Name]. I'm genuinely sorry to hear about your experience with our team — this is not the standard we hold ourselves to at [Business Name]. We take all feedback about our staff very seriously, and this will be addressed internally. Please reach out to me personally at [direct email] so I can apologize properly and make this right.
>
> — [Owner/Manager Name], [Business Name]

### G6 — Quality or Product Issue

> [Name], I'm so sorry to hear that [product/service] didn't meet your expectations. This isn't the outcome we want for any of our customers. We stand behind our work and would like to resolve this properly. Please contact us at [email/phone] and we'll arrange to [replace/redo/refund] as quickly as possible.
>
> — [Business Name]

### G7 — Misunderstanding or Expectation Gap

> Thank you for sharing your experience, [Name]. It sounds like there may have been a miscommunication about [service scope / pricing / timeline], and I sincerely apologize for that confusion. We should have done a better job explaining what was included upfront. We'd love the opportunity to clarify and make this right — please reach out to us at [contact] so we can speak directly.
>
> — [Manager Name], [Business Name]

### G8 — Negative Review with No Detail

> Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. We're sorry to see you had a less-than-positive experience. We'd love to understand what happened so we can improve. Please reach out to us at [email/phone] — we genuinely care about getting this right for you and every future customer.
>
> — [Business Name]

## Review Response Best Practices

### What To Always Do

1. Respond within 24-48 hours for all reviews (faster for negative reviews)
2. Use the reviewer's first name if available
3. Thank them for taking the time to write a review
4. Acknowledge the specific experience they mentioned
5. Sign off with a real name and title (not just the business name) for negative reviews
6. Keep responses under 150 words where possible — shorter responses are more authentic

### What To Never Do

1. Copy-paste the same response to every review — it looks automated and hurts credibility
2. Include personal or appointment details in public responses (privacy risk)
3. Argue or be defensive — even if the reviewer is wrong
4. Offer discounts or compensation publicly in a response (creates incentive fraud risk)
5. Ask the reviewer to change or remove their review in a public response
6. Respond while upset — write a draft, wait an hour, then review before posting

### Flagging Fake Reviews

If a review appears to be fake (no transaction record, suspicious profile, competitor pattern):

1. Flag the review in Google or Yelp using the "Report" function
2. Respond publicly but calmly (use Template G4)
3. Document with screenshots
4. Submit a formal dispute via Google Business Profile Help if the review violates policies
5. Do not use third-party services to have reviews removed — this risks account suspension

### Review Velocity

Sudden spikes in reviews can trigger platform filters that suppress new reviews temporarily. Aim for a steady organic flow rather than a burst campaign:

- Ask customers consistently rather than in campaigns
- Spread outreach via SMS, email, and in-person asks across the week
- Target 1-5 new reviews per week for most small businesses
- Do not use review gating (only sending review requests to customers you know are happy) — this violates Google and Yelp guidelines

## Review Monitoring Checklist

Set up monitoring so no review goes unresponded to:

- [ ] GBP notifications enabled (Settings > Notifications > New reviews)
- [ ] Yelp notifications enabled
- [ ] TripAdvisor notifications enabled (if applicable)
- [ ] Facebook notifications enabled
- [ ] Industry-specific platform notifications enabled
- [ ] Optional: Set up BrightLocal or Podium for centralized review management
