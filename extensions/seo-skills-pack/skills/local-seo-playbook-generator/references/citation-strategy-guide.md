# Citation Strategy Guide

Reference for building local citations by business category. Load this file when generating Tier 3 (category-specific) citation targets.

---

## Citation Tiers Explained

### Tier 1: Universal Foundations
Must-claim platforms for every local business regardless of category. Covered in main SKILL.md.

### Tier 2: Data Aggregators
Submit to aggregators first. Their data feeds hundreds of downstream directories, making them the most efficient citation investment.

| Aggregator | Submission Method | Coverage |
|-----------|------------------|----------|
| Data Axle (Infogroup) | Paid via partners or direct | ~200 directories |
| Neustar Localeze | Paid via partners | ~100 directories |
| Foursquare | Free self-submit | Apple Maps, Uber, Snapchat, hundreds more |
| Factual | Via Foursquare OS | Navigation apps, enterprise data buyers |

**Recommended tools:** Yext, Moz Local, BrightLocal — these submit to aggregators and many Tier 1/3 directories simultaneously.

### Tier 3: Category-Specific
See sections below for your business category.

### Tier 4: Local and Regional
- Local newspaper website business directory
- City or municipal government business directory
- Local Chamber of Commerce member listing
- Regional industry association member directory
- Neighborhood or community websites (Nextdoor, local Facebook groups)

## Citation Quality Signals

Not all citations are equal. Google weighs citation quality based on:

1. **Domain Authority of the directory**: Yelp, TripAdvisor, and niche industry sites outweigh generic directories
2. **NAP consistency**: Identical name, address, and phone with no variations
3. **Link to website**: Citations that include a dofollow link to the business website pass more value
4. **Category relevance**: A plumber listed on a home services directory is more valuable than the same listing on a generic business directory
5. **Profile completeness**: Full descriptions, photos, hours, and services on each platform strengthen the citation

## Citation Velocity

Build citations at a natural pace. Do not submit to hundreds of directories in a single day — sudden citation spikes can appear manipulative.

Recommended pace:
- Week 1: Claim and complete all Tier 1 profiles
- Week 2: Submit to all 4 aggregators
- Weeks 3-8: Add 3-5 Tier 3 citations per week
- Ongoing: Add 1-2 per month and audit for drift quarterly
