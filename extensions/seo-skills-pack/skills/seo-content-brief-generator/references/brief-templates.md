# Brief Templates: Heading Structure by Content Type

Reference templates for 8 common SEO content types. Use these as starting scaffolds, then adapt to the specific keyword and audience.

---

## Template 1: How-To Tutorial

Best for: "how to [do X]", "how to [achieve Y]", "[task] step by step"

```
H1: How to [Task]: [Benefit/Outcome in 4-6 words]

H2: What Is [Topic]? (skip if basic/obvious)
H2: Why [Benefit/Outcome] Matters
H2: What You'll Need Before You Start
  H3: [Prerequisite 1]
  H3: [Prerequisite 2]
H2: Step 1 -- [First Major Action]
  H3: [Sub-step or variation 1]
  H3: [Sub-step or variation 2]
H2: Step 2 -- [Second Major Action]
H2: Step 3 -- [Third Major Action]
  H3: [Common variation or context]
H2: Common Mistakes to Avoid
  H3: [Mistake 1]
  H3: [Mistake 2]
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
  H3: [Question about outcome]
  H3: [Question about prerequisites]
  H3: [Question about alternatives]
H2: Final Thoughts / Next Steps
```

Word count guide: 1,500 -- 3,000. More complex topics can go higher.

## Template 2: Listicle / Best-Of

Best for: "best [X] for [Y]", "top [N] [tools/strategies/examples]", "[N] ways to"

```
H1: The [N] Best [Topic] for [Audience/Use Case] in [Year]

H2: How We Evaluated [Topic]
  H3: [Evaluation criterion 1]
  H3: [Evaluation criterion 2]
H2: Quick Comparison Table
H2: 1. [Item Name] -- Best for [Specific Use Case]
  H3: Key Features
  H3: Pricing
  H3: Pros and Cons
H2: 2. [Item Name] -- Best for [Specific Use Case]
  H3: Key Features
  H3: Pricing
  H3: Pros and Cons
H2: 3. [Item Name] -- Best for [Specific Use Case]
[... repeat per item ...]
H2: How to Choose the Right [Item] for Your Needs
  H3: For [Audience Segment 1]
  H3: For [Audience Segment 2]
H2: Frequently Asked Questions
  H3: [Comparison question]
  H3: [Pricing/value question]
  H3: [Use-case specific question]
```

Word count guide: 150 -- 400 words per item + intro/closing sections.

## Heading Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any heading structure, verify:

- [ ] H1 includes primary keyword or close variation
- [ ] At least 2 H2s include secondary or semantic keywords naturally
- [ ] No two H2s cover the same subtopic
- [ ] H3s add genuine depth, not just restatements of the H2
- [ ] FAQ H2 is present (target for PAA and featured snippets)
- [ ] Heading order mirrors a reader's natural question progression
- [ ] No keyword stuffing in headings (max 1 keyword variant per heading)
- [ ] Each H2 could stand alone as a mini-article section
