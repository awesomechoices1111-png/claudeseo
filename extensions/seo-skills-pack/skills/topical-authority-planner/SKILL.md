# Topical Authority Planner

Creates a complete topical authority map for a given niche or topic. Defines pillar pages, supporting cluster posts, subtopics to cover, and the content relationships needed to signal comprehensive expertise to search engines.

## What to Collect Before Starting

- **Core topic or niche** — the main subject area (e.g., "email marketing", "personal finance for freelancers")
- **Domain URL** — to identify existing content and gaps
- **Business type** — what the site sells or promotes (affects commercial topic coverage)
- **Target audience** — beginner, intermediate, or expert readers
- **Content output capacity** — how many pieces per month? (affects roadmap scope)

---

## Step 1: Core Topic Definition

Define the topical territory to claim:

- **Head topic**: The overarching subject (e.g., "SEO")
- **Scope boundary**: What's in scope vs. out of scope (e.g., in: on-page SEO, off-page, technical; out: PPC, social media)
- **Audience intent spectrum**: What does a reader need to know from beginner to expert on this topic?

## Step 2: Topic Tree Construction

Map the full topic tree from head topic to supporting content:

```
HEAD TOPIC
├── Pillar 1: Major Subtopic A
│   ├── Cluster: Aspect 1 of A
│   ├── Cluster: Aspect 2 of A
│   └── Cluster: Aspect 3 of A
├── Pillar 2: Major Subtopic B
│   ├── Cluster: Aspect 1 of B
│   └── Cluster: Aspect 2 of B
└── Pillar 3: Major Subtopic C
    ├── Cluster: Aspect 1 of C
    └── Cluster: Aspect 2 of C
```

Each pillar page covers a major sub-area comprehensively. Each cluster post dives deep into one specific aspect.

## Step 3: Pillar Page Specifications

For each pillar page:
- **Target keyword**: Primary keyword (typically head term for the subtopic)
- **Intent**: Comprehensive guide / category hub
- **Length target**: 3,000-6,000 words
- **Coverage**: All major aspects of the subtopic summarized, linking to cluster posts
- **Internal linking**: Links to all associated cluster posts; receives links from all cluster posts

## Step 4: Content Gap Analysis

Identify what's missing from the current content against the full topic tree:
- Which pillar topics have no existing page?
- Which cluster subtopics are uncovered?
- Which user questions in the niche have no answer on the site?

Present gap as:
| Topic Area | Status | Priority |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Pillar: X | Missing | High |
| Cluster: Y | Exists (thin) | Medium |

## Step 5: Content Prioritization

Score each piece by:
- **Search volume** — higher = more traffic opportunity
- **Competition** — lower = easier to rank
- **Business value** — commercial vs. informational
- **Coverage depth** — does existing content need refresh vs. new creation?

**Priority matrix:**
- **Quick wins**: High volume + low competition + gap = build first
- **Authority builders**: High value + medium competition = build next
- **Long-term investments**: High competition + high value = build with strong internal linking support

## Step 6: Content Roadmap

Map content creation to a timeline:

| Month | Content Pieces | Type | Target Keyword |
|-------|---------------|------|----------------|
| 1 | X | Pillar | ... |
| 1 | Y | Cluster | ... |
| 2 | Z | Cluster | ... |

## Step 7: Interlinking Architecture

Define how pieces link together:
- Pillar links to each cluster post in the hub section
- Each cluster post links back to its pillar
- Cluster posts cross-link to 2-3 related clusters
- Use descriptive anchor text matching target keywords

## Output Format

Deliver:
1. **Topic tree** — full visual map of pillars, clusters, and supporting content
2. **Content gap report** — what exists vs. what's needed
3. **Priority list** — top 10 content pieces to create first
4. **Content roadmap** — 3-6 month content calendar
5. **Interlinking map** — which pages link to which
6. **Keyword assignments** — primary keyword for every planned piece
