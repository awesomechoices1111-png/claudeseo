# SEO & Keyword Optimization Skill

This skill provides guidance on keyword research, content optimization, and SEO strategy to help users improve their search engine visibility and attract qualified organic traffic. All recommendations are tailored to the specific domain and business context.

## Domain-Based Analysis Framework

When a user provides a domain name, use it to personalize all recommendations:

### Understanding the Domain Context

Before making SEO recommendations, establish:

1. **Domain type** - Identify whether it's a brand domain, niche blog, e-commerce site, SaaS product, local business, or informational resource
2. **Current authority** - Assess relative domain age, likely existing traffic patterns, and competitive positioning
3. **Niche & audience** - Determine the industry/niche, target customer persona, and geographic focus
4. **Business model** - Understand revenue model (e-commerce, affiliate, leads, subscriptions, ads, etc.) to align keyword strategy
5. **Existing visibility** - Consider what keywords the domain likely already ranks for based on content and authority level

### Domain-Specific Keyword Strategy

Tailor keyword research based on domain characteristics:

**For established domains with high authority:**
- Target competitive, high-volume keywords with strong commercial intent
- Focus on broad keyword categories and head terms
- Pursue ranking for keywords with higher CPC (cost-per-click) if monetizing
- Target informational keywords to build topical authority

**For new or lower-authority domains:**
- Prioritize long-tail keywords with lower competition
- Focus on keywords with moderate search volume but high intent match
- Target niche-specific, question-based keywords ("how to" and "what is")
- Build topical clusters around core themes before expanding

**For local/geographic domains:**
- Prioritize geo-modified keywords (city/region + main keyword)
- Target local intent keywords ("near me," "in [city]")
- Create location-specific content pages
- Emphasize local schema markup and NAP consistency

**For e-commerce domains:**
- Target product category keywords with commercial intent
- Include variations: "buy," "for sale," "best," "affordable"
- Focus on product comparison keywords
- Target long-tail product-specific searches

**For SaaS/B2B domains:**
- Prioritize product feature keywords and use case keywords
- Target comparison keywords ("vs," "alternative to," "best")
- Focus on ROI-driven keywords (time saved, efficiency gains)
- Include industry-specific terminology and acronyms

### Domain-Specific Content Gap Analysis

Analyze gaps with domain context:

1. **Authority-appropriate opportunities** - Recommend keywords the domain has realistic chance of ranking for
2. **Revenue alignment** - Prioritize gaps that align with the domain's business model and profit potential
3. **Content moat opportunities** - Identify underserved angles competitors miss where the domain could establish authority
4. **Quick wins** - Find gaps where minimal effort could generate rankings (low-hanging fruit)
5. **Strategic positioning** - Recommend gaps that support long-term authority building in the niche

### Domain-Based Competitive Analysis

When analyzing competitors for a specific domain:

1. **Identify direct competitors** - Rank domains competing for the same keywords in the user's niche
2. **Analyze competitor keyword strategy** - Determine which keywords competitors target, their content depth, and ranking positions
3. **Find uncontested gaps** - Identify high-value keywords competitors ignore or underserve
4. **Assess competitive difficulty** - Evaluate whether the user's domain can realistically outrank competitors for specific keywords
5. **Differentiation opportunities** - Recommend angles and formats competitors miss (video, tools, deeper guides, fresher data)

For domains facing strong competition, recommend:
- Targeting long-tail variations of competitor keywords
- Creating superior content (longer, better researched, more current)
- Building unique assets (tools, templates, original research, data)
- Earning topical authority through comprehensive content clusters
- Generating quality backlinks through original research or newsworthy angles

### Domain-Specific Ranking Potential Assessment

For each domain, provide realistic ranking timelines:

**Quick wins (1-3 months):**
- Keywords with low to very low competition
- Long-tail keywords under 100 monthly searches
- Keywords related to domain's existing authority areas
- Questions and how-to queries with 10-50 monthly searches

**Medium-term goals (3-6 months):**
- Moderate difficulty keywords in domain's niche
- Keywords with 100-500 monthly searches
- Emerging keywords with growing search interest
- Commercial keywords supporting business goals

**Long-term authority building (6+ months):**
- Competitive head-term keywords
- Keywords requiring topical authority and quality backlinks
- High-commercial-intent keywords with significant search volume
- Keywords requiring significant content investment and link building

## Core SEO Workflows

### Keyword Research & Analysis

When conducting keyword research, help users:

1. **Identify seed keywords** - Start with core terms related to the user's niche, products, or services
2. **Expand keyword variations** - Generate related keywords, long-tail variations, LSI keywords, and semantic variants
3. **Assess keyword metrics** - Evaluate search volume (relative popularity), keyword difficulty (competition level), and commercial intent
4. **Categorize by intent** - Classify keywords as informational (learning), navigational (brand-specific), commercial (buying signals), or transactional (ready to purchase)
5. **Prioritize opportunities** - Identify high-value keywords with reasonable search volume and lower competition that align with user goals

Provide keyword recommendations in clear, actionable lists with context about search volume, difficulty, and recommended content type.

### Search Intent Analysis

Understanding search intent is critical for content ranking success:

- **Informational intent**: User seeks knowledge or answers (how-to, definitions, tutorials)
- **Commercial intent**: User researches before purchasing (comparisons, reviews, best options)
- **Navigational intent**: User seeks a specific brand or website
- **Transactional intent**: User ready to take action (buy, download, sign up)

Match content strategy to the dominant search intent for each target keyword. Misaligned content won't rank well even with good keywords.

### Content Gap Analysis

Help users identify opportunities by:

1. **Analyzing target keywords** - Determine which keywords the user should rank for based on niche, audience, and business goals
2. **Auditing existing content** - Identify which keywords the user currently ranks for or has content addressing
3. **Finding gaps** - Spot opportunities where users rank poorly or have no content for valuable keywords
4. **Evaluating competitor content** - Review what competitors rank for to identify underserved angles
5. **Prioritizing gaps** - Recommend which gaps to fill first based on search volume, conversion potential, and effort

### Meta Description Optimization

Create effective meta descriptions that:

- Are 155-160 characters (optimal display length on most devices)
- Include primary keyword naturally, but don't force it
- Accurately describe page content
- Use active voice and compelling language
- Include a clear value proposition or benefit
- Avoid keyword stuffing or clickbait
- Match the search intent of target keywords

Example approach: "Focus on communicating the page's unique value and relevance to searchers, then naturally incorporate the primary keyword."

### On-Page Content Optimization

Guide users to optimize content for target keywords through:

- **Title tag optimization** - Include primary keyword within first 60 characters, keep compelling and descriptive
- **Heading hierarchy** - Use H1 for main topic (once), H2/H3 for subtopics, include keyword variations naturally
- **Body content** - Cover the topic comprehensively, use keyword variations and synonyms, maintain natural readability
- **Image optimization** - Use descriptive filenames and alt text with keyword context where relevant
- **URL structure** - Use descriptive, keyword-relevant URLs (e.g., /blog/keyword-research-guide vs. /blog/post123)
- **Internal linking** - Link to related content using keyword-rich anchor text to establish topic hierarchy

## SEO Best Practices

### Keyword Placement Strategy

Primary keywords should appear naturally in:
- H1 tag (once, naturally)
- First 100 words of content
- Meta description
- URL (if practical)

Avoid overuse - prioritize readability and user experience over keyword density.

### Content Structure for SEO

Recommend well-organized content that:
- Opens with an engaging summary that answers the query
- Uses clear headings to structure information
- Includes lists, tables, or visuals to break up text
- Thoroughly covers the topic (depth signals relevance)
- Concludes with a clear call-to-action or summary
- Targets 2,000+ words for competitive, informational queries (more depth = higher rankings often)

### Technical SEO Considerations

Mention these factors when relevant:
- **Page speed** - Faster loading improves rankings and user experience
- **Mobile optimization** - Over 60% of searches are mobile; responsive design is essential
- **SSL/HTTPS** - Security signals help rankings
- **Structured data** - Schema markup helps search engines understand content
- **Site architecture** - Logical URL structure and internal linking establish hierarchy
- **Core Web Vitals** - Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), First Input Delay (FID), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) impact rankings

## Domain-Specific Reporting & Metrics

### Contextualized Recommendations

When delivering SEO recommendations for a specific domain, always include:

1. **Domain positioning statement** - Brief assessment of current authority level, niche positioning, and competitive landscape
2. **Realistic success metrics** - Goals appropriate to domain age, authority, and industry competition (don't promise page 1 rankings for new domains competing in saturated niches)
3. **Timeline expectations** - Honest assessment of how long each recommendation will take to show results
4. **Resource requirements** - Content volume, link building effort, and technical work needed
5. **Revenue impact estimates** - Where relevant, estimate potential traffic and conversion impact

### Domain-Specific Reporting Examples

**For a 3-month-old blog targeting a niche:**
- Focus on quick-win keywords with lower competition
- Target 30-50 keywords over next 6 months
- Realistic goal: 500-2,000 monthly organic visitors within 6 months
- Recommend: 2-3 articles per week on targeted long-tail keywords

**For an established e-commerce site:**
- Target 50-100+ keywords across product categories
- Focus on product and commercial keywords
- Realistic goal: 5-15% increase in organic revenue within 3-6 months
- Recommend: Product page optimization + category guides + buyer intent content

**For a local business website:**
- Target 10-30 geo-modified local keywords
- Realistic goal: Top 3 rankings for primary local keywords within 2-3 months
- Recommend: Local citation building, review generation, local schema markup
- Expected impact: 20-50% increase in local search visibility

**For a SaaS company:**
- Target comparison keywords and high-intent use case keywords
- Realistic goal: 50-100 qualified leads per month from organic search
- Recommend: Product comparison content, use case guides, ROI calculators
- Expected impact: 15-30% of new customers from organic search

## Common SEO Challenges

When users mention struggles, consider the domain context:

- **Thin content** - Competitor content is more comprehensive; recommend expanding depth appropriate to domain's authority level
- **Unclear search intent** - Content targets wrong intent type; recommend content repositioning or new content better aligned with domain's niche
- **Poor keyword targeting** - Keywords are too competitive for domain's current authority or too low-volume; recommend keyword strategy shift
- **Technical issues** - Site structure or speed problems prevent ranking; recommend technical audits prioritized by domain type (e-commerce needs faster speed, local needs proper schema, SaaS needs better site architecture)
- **Authority gaps** - Site needs more quality backlinks to rank for competitive keywords; recommend link building strategy appropriate to domain's niche (guest posts, resource guides, partnerships)
- **Niche saturation** - Domain struggles in highly competitive niche; recommend either shifting to adjacent less-saturated niches or building unique authority angles
- **New domain handicap** - New domains face ranking challenges; recommend focusing on low-competition long-tail keywords until domain accumulates authority
- **Domain mismatch** - Domain authority doesn't align with keyword targets; recommend reassessing keyword strategy to match domain's realistic capabilities
- **Content vs. links imbalance** - Missing either quality content or quality backlinks; diagnose which is limiting factor for domain's specific situation
